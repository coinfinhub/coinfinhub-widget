CoinFinHub widgets

Show coin prices from CoinFinHub on any website: a price card, a list, a ticker and a converter. One script for all four, no dependencies, about 8 KB gzipped. Free, MIT-licensed code; prices for display, not for trading.

The widgets

Each in its light and dark look. theme="auto" follows the visitor's setting; light and dark fix it.

Price card <coinfinhub-price>

One coin's price and its 24-hour change; with chart="7d", the last 7 days as a line.

List <coinfinhub-list>

The top coins, or the ones you name, in rows.

Headline strip <coinfinhub-list layout="strip">

The same list as cells side by side, for the top of a page. They wrap on narrow screens.

Ticker <coinfinhub-ticker>

A strip that moves sideways in a loop. It stops while the pointer is over it or it has focus, and stands still (scroll it by hand) for visitors who ask for less motion.

Converter <coinfinhub-converter>

An amount of the coin and its value, both editable, in any of the 8 currencies. For display, not a quote.

Build your widget

Every change shows in the preview at once, and the code under it has only what differs from the defaults.

Coins

      Type to search the top coins, or type a coin's slug (the end of its page address, such as bitcoin) and press Enter. Find more coins on coinfinhub.com/widgets.

      Code

      Paste it where the widget should appear. The script is needed once per page, however many widgets it has. Without JavaScript, the plain link shows instead.

      <script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/coinfinhub/coinfinhub-widget@1.0.0/dist/coinfinhub-widget.js" integrity="sha384-0Gz84OlEq4cqspMQh7QgjMoVevKMUt7IOV4rVtWSqE/I/NK2nuSiMDHy5t2Ty/b/" crossorigin="anonymous"></script>
<coinfinhub-price coin="bitcoin"><a href="https://www.coinfinhub.com/price/bitcoin" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">CoinFinHub</a></coinfinhub-price>
