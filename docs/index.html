CoinFinHub price widget

Show a coin's price from CoinFinHub on any website: one script, one element, no dependencies, about 5 KB gzipped. Free, MIT-licensed code; prices for display, not for trading.

Add it to your page

Paste this where the price should appear. The coin is the end of its page address on coinfinhub.com, such as bitcoin in www.coinfinhub.com/price/bitcoin. Without JavaScript, the plain link shows instead.

<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/coinfinhub/coinfinhub-widget@1.0.0/dist/coinfinhub-price.js" integrity="sha384-FrZCDsGOc2NzaRF5IeeRF9oqaS5W8lGelYTR2b7VxuGwj3etd/2uoFh17ZHtr48D" crossorigin="anonymous"></script>
<coinfinhub-price coin="bitcoin" currency="USD" theme="auto"><a href="https://www.coinfinhub.com/price/bitcoin" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">CoinFinHub</a></coinfinhub-price>
